# Home & Dashboard

The Home screen is your creative command centre. Everything you need is one click away.

![Reica home dashboard — shortcuts and recent generations](./images/home-dashboard-overview.png)

---

## What you see on Home

The Home screen is split into two areas:

### Shortcuts (top section)
Quick-launch buttons for your primary workflows:

- **Still Life** — open a new product photography session
- **Board** — create a new composition canvas
- **Character** — go to your character library
- **Library** — open your asset manager
- **History** — view all your past generations

### Recent Generations (main area)
A scrollable grid of your most recent outputs. From here you can:

- **Click any image** to open it and reuse it as the base for a new generation
- See the generation details (date, settings used)
- Navigate directly to the feature used to create it

---

## Reusing a previous generation

One of the most powerful shortcuts: click any recent image on the Home screen and you're immediately taken to the generation form with that image pre-loaded.

This is how you iterate quickly:
1. Generate a first version
2. Return to Home
3. Click the image → the form opens with your previous settings
4. Adjust one variable and regenerate

---

## Navigation tips

- The sidebar (visible on all screens) gives you access to every tool at any time
- Your active Boards appear in the sidebar for quick switching
- The Library is always accessible without leaving your current generation
