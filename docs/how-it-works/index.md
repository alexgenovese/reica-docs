# How It Works

Reica is not a prompt-based AI. It's a **visual composition system** built on fashion-native AI models.

Here's what that means in practice.

---

## The core idea: references, not words

Most AI image tools require you to describe what you want in text ("a woman wearing a red dress on a white background, studio lighting, fashion editorial").

Reica works differently. You **show** what you want — upload a product, pick a character, choose a style — and the AI composes the result.

> Think of it like briefing a world-class photographer using a mood board, not words.

---

## The three layers of a generation

Every Reica output is built from three layers:

### 1. The Subject
Your product or garment. Reica reads shape, material, texture, and details from the image — automatically. Front + back upload gives the AI more information to work with.

### 2. The Character (optional)
A consistent AI model or a character you create. The same character can appear across hundreds of images with identical facial features, skin tone, and proportions — no re-briefing required.

### 3. The Creative Direction
Style, lighting, background, pose, camera angle, aspect ratio. These settings replace the photographer's direction without requiring you to know the technical terms.

---

## Why it's fashion-native

Generic AI models are trained on the entire internet. Reica's models are trained specifically on fashion, beauty and lifestyle imagery — which means:

- **Garment drape and fit** render realistically
- **Product textures** (leather, silk, knitwear) reproduce accurately
- **Skin tones and lighting** are optimised for editorial quality
- **Ghost mannequin and flat lay** outputs look like real studio shots

---

## Consistency across all outputs

The biggest challenge in fashion creative production is consistency across touchpoints. Reica solves this at the model level:

1. **Create a character once** — save it to your library
2. **Use it in any generation** — Still Life, Board, or batch
3. **Every output shares the same identity** — same face, body, and proportions

This means your Instagram campaign, your e-commerce PDP, and your lookbook can all feature the identical model with zero coordination overhead.

---

## Batch and scale

Reica is not just for one-off images. Use Batch Operations to:

- Apply a generation style to thousands of product images at once
- Generate a full seasonal catalogue from a single reference style
- Keep every SKU visually consistent without per-image manual work

---

## The output quality standard

Reica outputs at up to **4K resolution** — ready for print, billboard, or high-res digital. The AI is calibrated for "more real than reality": images that look like they were shot by a professional team, with premium lighting and styling.
