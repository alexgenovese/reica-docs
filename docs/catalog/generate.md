# Generate

You can generate one product from its page, or many products at once in a batch. Both use a brand profile; the cost in credits is shown before anything starts.

## One product: the product page

![A product page: photos and coverage on the left, still-life and on-model shots in the middle](/img/catalog/product-page.jpg)

The middle of the product page shows **Shots**: what this product has and what it could have, for still life and on-model. Each product starts with Front and Back; the other angles and poses are under **More angles** and **More poses**.

1. Click the shots that are missing. They join **Your images** on the right.
2. Check the setup. Each row says what it is set to now and opens its options:
   - **Still life setup**: Angles, Look (from the brand profile, or changed for this product), Quality.
   - **On-model setup**: Model, Poses, Outfit, Length (long dresses: Mid-thigh, Knee, Mid-calf, Ankle, or As in the photo), Length and fit, Look.
3. Read the cost and your balance, then choose **Generate N images**.

Changes made here are for this product only; the brand profile stays as it is.

The page follows the work: each shot shows Queued, Generating, then To review. You can keep adding shots while others are generating; they are queued after them.

:::info On-model starts from the approved front
An on-model photo is made from the product's approved front still life, which is the clean garment the model wears. Until the front still life is approved, on-model shots wait, marked "Needs an approved front". Generate the front, approve it, then make the on-model shots.
:::

A shot no photo shows, such as a back view without a back photo, is marked "No back photo": Reica will imagine that side. Add the photo first if the back matters.

## Many products: a batch

A batch is several products generated together with one brand profile. Go to **Batches**, choose **New batch**, then **Generation**.

### 1. Products

Pick products from your folders, seasons and collections (**+** adds a whole folder), search by name or SKU, or filter on **Never generated** and **Nothing approved**. Your plan sets how many products a batch can hold, and the wizard says so.

### 2. Brand profile

![The brand profile step of a new batch, with the products table](/img/catalog/batch-profile.jpg)

Pick the profile. Every product gets the same shots, and a summary shows what they will be: angles, background, light, shadow, margin, format, quality, poses and the quality threshold. You can change the on-model outfits for this batch only.

The table lists each product with its category, coverage and shots. A product that cannot be made yet says why: no front photo, no category, already generating, its angles already approved, or its on-model shots waiting for an approved front.

### 3. Delivery and start

![The last step of a new batch: quality, Fast or Standard, and the summary with the credits](/img/catalog/batch-delivery.jpg)

**Quality of the still lifes**: 1K for the shop, 2K for zoom, 4K for print and campaigns. On-model shots are always 1K.

**How fast**:

| | Fast | Standard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| When | About the time shown, images arrive one by one | Most within 24 hours |
| Price | Full price | A discount on the still lifes, larger as the batch grows |
| Quality | 1K, 2K or 4K | 1K only |
| Size | Up to 50 products | Any size |

Over 50 products, a batch is made as Standard at 1K. Make 2K or 4K versions of the approved images after review. On-model shots are always made as Fast.

**Before you start** lists what you should know: another batch running (one batch runs at a time, this one starts after it), on-model shots waiting for a front, sides that will be imagined. Approved shots are kept, unless you tick **Make them again**.

The summary shows the shots, the delivery, the credits and your balance after. Choose **Start batch**.

Credits are taken as each image starts, and returned for any image that fails.

### The batch page

![A finished batch with its counters and the Quality check panel listing two shots to fix](/img/catalog/batch-page.jpg)

The batch page shows how far it is: done, to review, approved, not done, shots under the threshold and credits (with what was refunded). Products that need attention come first.

- **Cancel batch** stops the shots that have not started.
- **Retry N not done** makes the failed and skipped shots again.
- **Review N** opens the images waiting for you.
- **Export approved** and **Send to Google Drive** appear once something is approved.

When the batch is finished, a **Quality check** panel lists the images still under the threshold with what the check found. Untick the ones you want to keep as they are and choose **Fix N shots**: each one is edited to fix what was found, checked again and sent back to Review.

## High resolution

An approved still life or on-model photo can get a 2K or 4K version for print, zoom and campaigns: from the shot's Download menu or the High resolution section in Review. It is drawn again with finer fabric detail, is ready in about a minute and does not go back to review.
