## What's included in every plan

Regardless of the plan you choose, every Reica user gets:

- ✅ Fashion, beauty and lifestyle native AI models
- ✅ No Prompt composition (image-based generation)
- ✅ At least one Character slot
- ✅ Library with folder organisation
- ✅ History with full generation details
- ✅ Export up to 4K resolution
- ✅ Ghost mannequin, flat lay and on-model output types

## Frequently asked pricing questions

### How are generations counted?
Each final image you generate counts as one generation credit. Generating multiple variations in the same session counts once per output.

### Can I upgrade or downgrade at any time?
Yes. Plan changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle with no lock-in.

### Is there a free trial?
Refer to [getreica.com](https://getreica.com) for current trial and onboarding offers.

### Can my whole team use Reica?
Yes. Team and Enterprise plans include multiple seats, shared Libraries, shared Characters, and admin controls.

### What file formats can I upload?
JPEG, PNG, and WebP are supported for product and reference image uploads.

### What's the maximum export resolution?
Up to 4K (3840 × 2160px or equivalent aspect ratios).
