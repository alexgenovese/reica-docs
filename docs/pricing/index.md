# Pricing

Reica is paid with credits. Each plan gives you credits every month and sets what you can do with them: how many products per batch, how many brand profiles, which image quality.

## Free trial

Every new account starts with a free trial, with no card:

- 14 days and 20 credits;
- batches of up to 5 products, Fast delivery only;
- downloads carry a light "Reica trial" mark. The images in Reica stay clean.

When the trial or its credits end, you choose a plan, monthly or yearly.

## What things cost

The exact cost is always shown before you start, on the Generate button or in the batch summary. Credits are counted to the tenth, so a balance or a price can read 1.5 or 12.5. These are the usual prices:

| Action | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| Still life at 1K | 2 |
| Still life at 2K | 3 |
| Still life at 4K | 4.5 |
| On-model photo (always 1K) | 4 |
| Try again on a shot that failed | The price of the shot |
| An edit of a shot (see below) at 1K | 1 |
| An edit of a 2K still life | 1.5 |
| 2K version of an approved shot | 1.5 |
| 4K version of an approved shot | 2.3 |

A 2K image costs 1.5 times the 1K price and a 4K image 2.25 times, rounded up to the next tenth of a credit.

**Included in every image**: the quality check, and one automatic fix when an image falls under your threshold.

### Edits

An *edit* is a new version of a still life or an on-model photo made by changing the image you have: **Fix with AI** and the quick fixes in the editor, **Length and fit**, replacing a garment, **Regenerate** with your notes or marked spots in Review, and **Fix N shots** at the end of a batch. It costs less than a new image.

Each plan includes edits every calendar month (the table above: 10, 25 and 30). While one is left the button says "Included, 7 of 10 left this month", and the edit costs nothing. When they are used up, an edit costs the Edit price, shown on the button: 1 credit at 1K. The count starts again on the first of the month. An edit that fails gives its place back. A free trial has no included edits.

Not edits: the automatic fix of an image under your threshold (it is part of the image), the length and fit that Reica applies on its own to an on-model photo, and trying again on a shot that failed.

[Placing the label](/catalog/review#place-the-label) on an image is free. Sewing it into a 2K version is an edit: one included edit or 1.5 credits, given back if Reica keeps the flat label.

**Free**: adding products and importing feeds, reading the look of a photo, cleaning your label photo, placing the label on an image, saving a version from the editor, checking an image with [Verify image](/compliance#verify-an-image), exports and transfers to Google Drive.

**Standard delivery** costs less: still lifes made at 1K in Reica's batch are 20% off, 25% from 100 still lifes, 30% from 300, 33% from 1,000.

**Refunds**: a batch sets aside its whole price when it starts, so it cannot stop half way for lack of credits. Each image is paid from it as it starts. What an image did not use goes back when the batch ends, and the credits of any image that fails are returned. A high-resolution version that does not match the approved image, or changes the text on the label, is not kept and is refunded.

## Questions

### Can I upload any file format?

JPG, PNG and WebP, up to 15 MB each. Photos of at least 2000 px on the long side give the best results.

### What is the highest resolution?

4K for still lifes on the Pro plan. On-model photos are made at 1K; once approved, they can get a 2K or 4K version on Growth and Pro.

### Where do I see my credits?

At the foot of the sidebar in Reica, and in Settings, under Billing.
