# Pricing

Reica is paid with credits. Each plan gives you credits every month and sets what you can do with them: how many products per batch, how many brand profiles, which image quality.

**Enterprise**: more products per batch, unlimited brand profiles and dedicated support, on a contract. Contact us from [getreica.com](https://getreica.com).

## Free trial

Every new account starts with a free trial, with no card:

- 14 days and 20 credits;
- batches of up to 5 products, Fast delivery only;
- downloads carry a light "Reica trial" mark. The images in Reica stay clean.

When the trial or its credits end, you choose a plan, monthly or yearly.

## What things cost

The exact cost is always shown before you start, on the Generate button or in the batch summary. These are the usual prices:

| Action | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| Still life at 1K | 2 |
| Still life at 2K | 3 |
| Still life at 4K | 5 |
| On-model photo (always 1K) | 4 |
| Regenerate with your notes, or try again | 2 |
| 2K version of an approved shot | 2 |
| 4K version of an approved shot | 3 |

**Included in every image**: the quality check, and one automatic fix when an image falls under your threshold.

**Free**: adding products and importing feeds, reading the look of a photo, saving a version from the editor, exports and transfers to Google Drive.

**Standard delivery** costs less: still lifes made at 1K in Reica's batch are 20% off, 25% from 100 still lifes, 30% from 300, 33% from 1,000.

**Refunds**: credits are taken as each image starts, and returned for any image that fails.

## Questions

### Can I upload any file format?

JPG, PNG and WebP, up to 15 MB each. Photos of at least 2000 px on the long side give the best results.

### What is the highest resolution?

4K for still lifes on the Pro plan. On-model photos are made at 1K; once approved, they can get a 2K or 4K version on Growth and Pro.

### Where do I see my credits?

At the foot of the sidebar in Reica, and in Settings, under Billing.
